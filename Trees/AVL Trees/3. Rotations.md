AVL trees are special type of BSTs that re-balance themselves, every-time we add or remove values from them. 

They do this by ensuring that the difference in the height of the left and right sub-trees is never more than 1. If the difference is more than one, they rebalance themselves using rotations.

Which rotation we use depends on what side of the tree is heavy.
